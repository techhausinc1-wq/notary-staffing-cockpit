What This Platform Does

A CRM gives you a place to record what happened. This is built to tell you what needs to happen next, and do part of the work for you. The workflows you can click through here (bookings, roster, matching, notes, lead tracking, the Copilot layer below) are genuinely working, not static mockups. Two honest exceptions: Scout uses illustrative sample prospects rather than a live business directory, and the integrations below marked "Demo-Only" are simulated inside this app, not wired to a real inbox or calendar yet. Both are real next steps, not already-built.

The part a CRM doesn't do
Copilot Briefing
Every time you load the dashboard, it re-scans every booking, request, and lead and tells you what's actually urgent today — not a report you have to go build yourself.
Smart-Ranked Assignment
Assigning a job isn't a plain list anymore — candidates are ranked by current workload, coverage area, and track record on similar work, with the reasoning shown. You still pick — it just does the thinking first.
One-Click Message Drafting
Open any booking or request and draft a client confirmation or an assignment text in one click, pre-filled from the real record — copy it or send it, instead of typing it from scratch every time.
Notary Scout
Finds new referral-source prospects (title companies, law firms, lenders) in a zip code and drafts the outreach — a CRM stores contacts, it doesn't go find you new ones.
Core modules running in this build
Booking & Assignment Engine
Requests come in, get assigned to a vetted person, tracked through to completion — the Notary Bookings and VA Requests boards.
Vetted Roster & Trust Layer
One pool of people who passed inspection, shared across both lines of business — the Vetted Talent Pool view.
Lead & Channel Tracking
Every inquiry tagged by source — paid search, referral, repeat client — so you know what's actually generating business.
Email & Document Automation
Confirmations go out automatically, signed documents file themselves against the booking that produced them.
